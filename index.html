DSH Mail Assistant

A personal email management tool

About

DSH Mail Assistant is a private, personal-use desktop utility. It helps the account owner manage their own mailbox: sending outgoing emails, and reading incoming messages such as replies, delivery status notifications and bounce-backs, so that email correspondence can be handled efficiently from one place.

This application is not distributed to the public. It is operated solely by its developer for their own email accounts.

Features

  • Send emails from the owner's own mailbox
  • Read incoming replies, out-of-office notices and bounce notifications
  • Keep simple records of correspondence status
